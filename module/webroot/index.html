Restrictions

Open an app on Home to block its internet or freeze it. Anything you restrict shows up here.

What do they do?

Block internet
Adds a firewall rule for the app. Re-applied after every reboot.
Limit background
Stops the app and stops it running in the background. It still opens normally.
Freeze (disable)
Fully disables the app until you unfreeze it. Essential system apps are protected.

Recording

Pause recording
Stops the recorder completely.
Scan every
Longer means cooler and less battery.
Hide system entries
Skips low-level system processes.
Log size limit
Oldest events are dropped past this.

Display

Lens refraction
Magnifies and bends light in the bottom lens while you press and drag it. Turn off if it looks odd.
Auto-refresh
Updates this page every 30 seconds.
